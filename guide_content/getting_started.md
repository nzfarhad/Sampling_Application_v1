# Getting Started

The ATR Sampling Tool helps you choose a sampling method, work out the sample size and draw a documented sample. Each tab does one job:

| Tab | Use it to |
|-----|-----------|
| Decision Tree | Get a recommended sampling design for your study, then send it to a calculator or the Sample Generator |
| Sample Calculator | Size a monitoring survey: a target margin of error for a proportion or a mean |
| Evaluation Calculator | Size a comparison between groups or survey rounds, or find the smallest difference a sample can detect |
| Sample Generator | Upload your sampling frame and draw the sample, with weights and a methodology record |
| Sampling Guideline | Read about sampling methods, quality control and field procedures |

## A Typical Workflow

1. **Choose the approach.** Answer the questions in the Decision Tree, or go straight to a calculator if you already know the design.
2. **Calculate the sample size.** Use the Sample Calculator for a single survey round, or the Evaluation Calculator when you will compare groups or rounds.
3. **Draw the sample.** Upload the frame in the Sample Generator, review the frame checks, then confirm the draw.
4. **Document and send to the field.** Download the Excel file. It holds the sample with weights, a Methodology sheet and a Replacement Log for the field team.

The Study Name you type in one tab is shared with the others, and it appears in every download.

## Data Protection

The app runs on a public cloud host. Upload anonymised frames only: no names, phone numbers or GPS points. Keep the full list offline, and link it back to the sample through the unit ID.
