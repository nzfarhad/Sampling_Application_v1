# Best Practices

## Before Starting Your Study

- Define the research objectives and the target population clearly.
- Understand your data collection constraints and resources.
- Decide the precision you need for the estimates that matter.
- Plan for non-response and data quality problems.

## When Using the Tools

- Check every input before you calculate or draw.
- Use confidence levels and margins of error that match how the results will be used.
- Draw reserves (a buffer) for replacements, and use them in the order given.
- Record your sampling decisions and the reasons for them; the Methodology sheet helps.

## Quality Assurance

- Check that the frame covers the target population, and record what it misses.
- Monitor response rates during fieldwork and act on problems early.
- Log every replacement and every deviation from the sampling plan.

This tool gives statistical guidance. Consult a sampling specialist for complex studies or difficult contexts.
