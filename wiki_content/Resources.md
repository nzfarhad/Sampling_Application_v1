# Recommended Textbooks

## Essential Sampling Books


#### **Sampling: Design and Analysis** by Sharon L. Lohr
- **Publisher**: Cengage Learning
- **Edition**: 3rd Edition (2019)
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Focus**: Comprehensive coverage of sampling theory and practice
- **Strengths**: Clear explanations, practical examples, R code
- **Best For**: Graduate students and practitioners

#### **Sampling for monitoring and evaluation** by Chris Scott
- **Publisher**: Wold Bank
- **Edition**: 1985
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Focus**: A Technical Supplement to Monitoring and Evaluation of Agriculture and Rural Development Projects
- **Strengths**: Focused on monitoring and evaluation
- **Best For**: M&E Profissionals
- **Link**: <a href="https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/304901468765592800/pdf/multi-page.pdf" target="_blank">Sampling for monitoring and evaluation</a>
