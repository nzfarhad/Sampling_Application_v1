# Recommended Books

#### **Sampling: Design and Analysis** by Sharon L. Lohr
- **Publisher**: CRC Press (Chapman & Hall/CRC)
- **Edition**: 3rd Edition (2021, copyright 2022)
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Focus**: Comprehensive coverage of sampling theory and practice
- **Strengths**: Clear explanations, practical examples, R and SAS companion material
- **Best For**: Graduate students and practitioners

#### **Survey Sampling** by Leslie Kish
- **Publisher**: Wiley
- **Edition**: 1st Edition (1965, reprinted 1995)
- **Level**: Intermediate to Advanced
- **Focus**: Classic treatment of survey sampling
- **Strengths**: Theoretical foundation, comprehensive coverage
- **Best For**: Those seeking deep theoretical understanding

#### **Sampling for Monitoring and Evaluation** by Chris Scott
- **Publisher**: World Bank
- **Edition**: 1985
- **Level**: Intermediate
- **Focus**: A Technical Supplement to Monitoring and Evaluation of Agriculture and Rural Development Projects
- **Strengths**: Focused on monitoring and evaluation
- **Best For**: M&E Professionals
- **Link**: <a href="https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/304901468765592800/pdf/multi-page.pdf" target="_blank">Sampling for Monitoring and Evaluation (PDF)</a>

# Field Data Collection Tools

#### **KoboToolbox**
Free, open-source forms and mobile data collection, widely used by humanitarian organisations. Works offline on Android phones and tablets.

<a href="https://www.kobotoolbox.org/" target="_blank">kobotoolbox.org</a>

#### **SurveyCTO**
Paid platform for secure mobile and web forms, with offline collection, built-in data quality checks and encryption.

<a href="https://www.surveycto.com/" target="_blank">surveycto.com</a>

Both tools can load the sample list exported by the Sample Generator (for example as a pre-loaded CSV), so enumerators only see the units selected for interview.
